The sentiment analysis system classifies citizen complaints into positive, negative and neutral categories.

The extractive summariser selects important sentences based on frequently occurring words.

The manual evaluation compares predicted sentiment with human judgement. Differences occur because the rule-based system uses a limited vocabulary and cannot fully understand context, sarcasm or complex expressions.

The SQLite composite index on station and day helps improve filtered query performance.

For a larger city monitoring network, SQLite can support an initial implementation. A larger database system and machine learning-based sentiment model may be considered as data volume increases.
